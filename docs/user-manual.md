# User manual

To get the app running, see [Setup & run](../README.md#setup--run) or [Deploy](../README.md#deploy). The seeded test accounts are listed in the README.

## Browse questions (no account needed)

1. Open the start page to see the latest questions.
2. Narrow the list with the **School**, **Program** and **Course** filters. Choosing a school limits the programs and courses to that school.
3. Type in **Search questions** to find questions whose title or description contains your words.
4. Click a question to open it and read its answers.

## Create an account and log in

1. Click **Sign up**, then fill in your full name, email and password, and choose your school and program.
2. You are logged in right away. Next time, use **Log in** with your email and password.
3. You stay logged in until you log out (from the profile menu or the sidebar) or the session expires after 7 days.

## Ask a question

1. Click **Ask a question** in the navbar.
2. Choose a **Course**, then write a **Title** and a **Description**.
3. Submit. The question shows up in the list for everyone who filters on that course, school or program.

## Answer a question

1. Open a question.
2. Write in **Your answer** and submit. The answer is added below the question.
3. The question's author gets a notification.

## Notifications

- The bell in the navbar shows how many unread notifications you have. It updates by itself every 30 seconds.
- Click the bell to see the list. Unread notifications are bold.
- Click a notification to mark it as read and open the question.

## Edit your profile

1. Open **Profile** from the avatar menu.
2. Edit your full name, school and program, then save.
3. Email and role can't be changed.

## Delete questions and answers

- You can delete your own questions and answers. The delete button only shows up on content you're allowed to delete.
- Moderators can delete any question or answer.
- Deleting a question also deletes all of its answers.
